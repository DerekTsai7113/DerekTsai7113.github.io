My work centers on statistical machine learning for scientific and biomedical problems, with particular interests in neural data, reliable prediction, and representation learning. Below are selected projects; each page gives a short explanation of the research question, methods, and my contribution.

## [Speech–Brain Representation Alignment](/projects/speech-brain-alignment/)

**Academia Sinica · Research Internship, 2026**

I study how representations from pretrained speech and language models correspond to human neural responses measured with EEG and fMRI. The project uses neural encoding models and layer-wise representation analysis to compare model representations across clean and noisy speech conditions.

[Project details →](/projects/speech-brain-alignment/)

---

## [fMRI Cross-Condition Speech Decoding](/projects/fmri-cross-condition-decoding/)

**Academia Sinica · Ongoing Research Collaboration**

This collaboration examines whether speech-related information can be decoded from fMRI across noisy and speech-enhanced listening conditions. I contribute to model development, ROI-wise evaluation, baseline comparisons, and statistical analysis for cross-condition decoding.

[Project details →](/projects/fmri-cross-condition-decoding/)

---

## [Cause-of-Death Modeling](/projects/cause-of-death-modeling/)

**University of Washington · Research Assistant, 2025**

I developed machine-learning pipelines for automated cause-of-death classification using verbal-autopsy and related clinical data. The work focused on hierarchical prediction, severe class imbalance, heterogeneous inputs, and leakage-safe evaluation.

[Project details →](/projects/cause-of-death-modeling/)

---

## [Adaptive Conformal Prediction for Chaotic Time Series](/projects/adaptive-conformal-prediction/)

**University of Washington · Graduate Course Project**

I investigated why standard split conformal prediction can lose coverage in closed-loop chaotic forecasting and evaluated adaptive calibration strategies based on rolling windows and exponentially weighted updates.

[Project details →](/projects/adaptive-conformal-prediction/)
